About WishKind

WishKind is a concept for a community wishlist platform where people can explain their goals and, in a future version, receive support from others.

Kindness with transparency

A real launch must explain where money goes, protect people's personal information, prevent fraud, and verify payments and recipients responsibly.

Supporting people in need

The long-term vision includes making it easier for people facing hardship, including Palestinian communities, to share needs and find legitimate support. Actual fundraising and payouts depend on lawful payment routes and provider approval.
